HP left after the fight

Rows are attackers, columns are defenders. Each side's time to kill gets the approach time for the shorter-range unit added (range gap ÷ its speed); the faster effective TTK wins. The winner's remaining HP reuses the TTK hit maths: each loser volley delivers loser models × splash target hit-slots while in range (a shorter-range loser only fires after its approach), each winner model needs ceil(model HP ÷ damage per attack) hits to drop with overkill on the killing hit wasted, leftover hits only scratch the next survivor. Cell colour shows the fraction of the winner's own squad HP that survives (green = flawless, red = barely won, grey = mutual destruction). Hover a cell for the working.
