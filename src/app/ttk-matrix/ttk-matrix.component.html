Rows are attackers, columns are defenders, computed in the browser straight from Unit Stats.csv. Each cell is the time to destroy the defender's whole squad (shown capped at {{ cap }}s): hits per model = ceil(model HP / damage per attack), multiplied by the number of models in the defender's squad to get hit-slots needed, divided by the attacker's squad size times the number of models its splash covers (models whose square unit width bodies overlap the circular blast of radius splash/2, capped at the squad size) to get attack ticks, then multiplied by the attack interval. Splash deals full damage with no falloff to every model in the blast. Overkill on the last hit against each model is wasted and does not carry over to the next model. Hover a cell for the working, click a column header to sort. Cells shown as -- cannot engage: a ground-only attacker cannot hit an air defender.
